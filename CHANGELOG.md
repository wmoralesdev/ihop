## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-29

### Added

- Interactive views for web apps, all open ports, recent history, and details.
- Direct `list`, `details`, `recent`, and port-closing commands.
- TCP and UDP discovery on macOS, Linux, and Windows.
- Friendly process, launcher, project, exposure, age, CPU, and memory metadata.
- Graceful termination with explicit force escalation and PID revalidation.
- Sanitized process details and private OS-standard kill history.
